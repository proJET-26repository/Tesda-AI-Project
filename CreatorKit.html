README.txt & Quick Guide

Welcome to CreatorKit — your all-in-one local-first content creation suite designed for YouTubers, Reels creators, TikTokers, and AI artists.

Features Included:

  • Prompt Generator: Create AI art, video, and character prompts using smart templates. Fully editable with copy & save actions.
  • Video Idea Generator: Explore random viral video concepts across motivational, nature, AI cartoons, food, and promotions.
  • Caption Maker: Generate tailored hooks, captions, CTAs, and hashtags for Shorts, Reels, TikTok, and Instagram.
  • Saved Vault: Store prompts, ideas, and captions in browser `localStorage` with search and delete functions.
  • Content Planner: Manage your publishing schedule, status (Idea, In Progress, Completed), and dates.

How to Run:

Simply save this file as CreatorKit.html and open it directly in any modern web browser. No server or database required!
